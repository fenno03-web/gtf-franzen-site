Power
and infra

Power plan, power distribution, truss and rigging. So everything on site keeps running all day.

Discuss a projectRental

In an empty field, everything starts with power. So that is where we start too.

We draw up a power plan in advance: what needs power where, how much, and how the cables run safely across the site. Generators come from trusted partners. Distribution and connections we do ourselves.

We also build truss, towers and rigging for stages and lighting rigs, and lay the network for sound, lighting and video: fibre, CAT6 and Art-Net.

How we work

01

Calculate first

We list everything that needs power. That way there is enough, and nothing fails at the busiest moment.

02

Neatly distributed

Cables run where they should, with distribution boxes in logical places. Safe for the public, easy for everyone who needs to plug in.

03

One point of contact

Power, sound, lighting and public address from one company. Less coordination, before and on the day.

Frequently asked questions
