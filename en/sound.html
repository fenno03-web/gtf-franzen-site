Sound

Line arrays, monitoring and operation for stages, tents and sites.

Discuss a projectRental

From a marquee for a thousand people to a festival stage: we make sure every word lands and it sounds right everywhere.

It all started in Bocholtz with sound: rental and home-built speakers. Today we work with systems from d&b audiotechnik and Dynacord, and mix on a Soundcraft Vi1 with a fibre stagebox.

We tune the system to the room, the audience and the programme. One of our engineers is at the desk from soundcheck to the last act.

  • d&b audiotechnik E8
  • Dynacord Cobra 4
  • Dynacord Madras M18
  • Soundcraft Vi1
  • Bose 802
  • Dynacord M12
What you get
  • System design matched to venue and audience
  • Line arrays, subs and front fills
  • Monitoring for stage and DJ
  • Operation by our own engineers
  • Wireless microphones and headsets
  • Set-up, soundcheck and take-down

How we work

01

Before

We look at the venue and the programme and design the system, so coverage is right before anything goes up.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book sound only?

Yes. Every service can be booked on its own. Clients often combine sound with lighting or public address so it is all in one hand.

Does an engineer come along?

For events, yes. One of our engineers runs the system from soundcheck to the end.

Can I hire sound equipment without an engineer?

For simple set-ups, yes. Have a look at the rental catalogue or ask us.
