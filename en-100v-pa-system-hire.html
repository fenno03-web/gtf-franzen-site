CH 01100V public address

GTF Franzen from Bocholtz (South Limburg, the Netherlands) hires out 100V public address systems for events. We own more than 300 horn speakers, mostly Philips, plus 100V amplifiers, microphones, cabling and masts. We plan the layout, set it up, operate it if you like and take it down again. We work across the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany.

Public address over large sites and long routes is what we have been known for over generations. You will recognise us by the horns that read FRANZEN BOCHOLTZ. Along the Amstel Gold Race course they make sure the crowd hears what is happening in the race.

?Frequently asked questions

Can I hire a 100V public address system from you?

Yes. We hire out complete 100V PA systems from our own stock: horn speakers, 100V amplifiers, microphones, cables and masts. We usually set up ourselves so you know everything works.

What is a 100V public address system?

A system where many speakers are connected to a single amplifier over long cables. It is reliable and ideal for large sites, routes and buildings.

Where do you work?

From Bocholtz we cover the whole of the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany. Aachen, Liège and Maastricht are all close by.

How much does it cost to hire a 100V PA system?

That depends on the number of speakers, the distances and the hire period. You always get a free, no-obligation quote.
